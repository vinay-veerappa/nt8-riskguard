- [MINOR] OnFollowerOrderUpdate: The verification block acquires `_lock` twice in succession — once to look up the bracket and identify the leg, then again inside the `if (verifyBracket != null)` block to read/clear the requested fields. This is not a real-world issue since stops never have quantity 0, but it is a latent fragility.
